# Figure captions

## Aggregate results
Reported test-set performance of tabular DNN and DeepInsight + CNN pipelines on NSL-KDD and CICMalDroid20. Values transcribed from Table 4 (p. 1235) of AL-Essa et al. (2026), DOI: 10.11591/eei.v15i2.9649. Bars show Weighted F1, Macro F1 and overall accuracy on a common 0–1 scale. Values are rounded as published. These are reported results, not new experimental replications; no uncertainty estimates are available. Both representation and model architecture differ between pipelines.

## Per-class results
Per-class test F1 transcribed from the numeric labels in Figure 1 (p. 1235) of the same paper. All panels use a zero baseline. CICMalDroid20 SMSMalware and Riskware improve in the image pipeline, despite lower aggregate metrics. Source labels are retained. Values are rounded as published; no uncertainty estimates are available.
